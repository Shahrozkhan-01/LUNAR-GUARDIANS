# User Manual

Welcome, Commander! All numbers that change the game are **SIMULATED GAME
UNITS**. NASA information is shown for learning.

## 1. Open the game

Run `.\start-lunar-guardians.ps1`, then open the displayed frontend address.
The launcher starts both local services. If PowerShell blocks the script, use
`Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass`.

## 2. Start a mission

Click **Start Mission**. Choose the available Lunar Outpost destination.

## 3. Choose difficulty

- **Explorer:** gentler challenge and more protection.
- **Commander:** balanced training.
- **Expert:** tighter reserves and harder decisions.

## 4. Choose crew size

Two astronauts use fewer daily supplies. Four is balanced. Six uses more
oxygen, water, and food.

## 5. Choose mission duration

Pick 5, 10, or 15 simulated days. Longer missions need more planning.

## 6. Choose a NASA briefing

Read and select one:

- **South Polar Exploration:** Diviner and LOLA context.
- **Space Weather Preparedness:** ACE EPAM and STEREO-A context.
- **Communication and Navigation:** Horizons range and light-time context.

These are learning presets. Different NASA records may have different dates.

## 7. Understand resources

Mission Control shows Oxygen, Water, Power, Food, and Shield protection.
Bars and labels show current simulated levels. Crew health and subsystem cards
show the effect of your choices. NASA measurements are not resource values.

## 8. Advance mission days

Review the dashboard, resolve required events when present, then click
**NEXT DAY**. Daily consumption, generation, recycling, crops, and events use
the existing simulation rules.

## 9. Handle normal events

Read the event description and choose one of the available responses. The
result explains the simulated resource or Science Point change.

## 10. Use NASA Training Events

Training Events are optional. Read the NASA context and limitations, then pick
one response. They are labeled `SIMULATED TRAINING EVENT`; their effects are
`SIMULATED GAME CONSEQUENCE`.

## 11. Understand NASA alerts

The NASA Space Weather panel shows connection status, update time, event
counts, source records, dates, and links. `REAL NASA DATA` is not a warning
that measures your outpost dose. If NASA is unavailable, the mission remains
playable and no fake events are shown.

## 12. Complete a mission

Keep the crew and essential resources in safe ranges until the final day.
Missions can succeed, fail, or finish with difficulties.

## 13. Read the final debrief

The debrief shows the outcome, final day, simulated Science Points score, final
resources, what went well, what to improve, lessons, important decisions, and
major events.

## 14. Restart

Click **Relaunch Day 1** for a fresh version of the same mission, or choose
**Setup** to configure a new mission. Resources, decisions, events, and the
old debrief do not carry into the new mission.

## 15. Sound controls

Use **SOUND OFF/SOUND ON** in the navigation or Mission Control header. Sound
is off by default. When enabled, short generated tones play for hover, clicks,
selection, launch, success, warnings, and completion. The preference is saved
in your browser. No background music plays automatically.
